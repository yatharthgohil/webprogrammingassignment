01

The story

I created The Watch Journal for a simple reason: I like watches. There is something remarkable about an everyday object that combines style, engineering, and personality.

A watch can be practical without being ordinary. The shape of the hands, the texture of a dial, and the movement working inside all play a part. Two watches can do the same basic job and still look and feel completely different.
