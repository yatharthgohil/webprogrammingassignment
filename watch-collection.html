03 / A Watch Collection

Different watches.
Different stories.

A watch collection can start with just one piece that means something to you. A gift, a reminder of a special day, or a watch you saved up for can be more interesting than a drawer full of things you never wear.

Learning about movements is part of the fun. A quartz watch uses an electronic oscillator regulated by a quartz crystal. A mechanical watch runs on a wound spring, and an automatic watch is a mechanical watch that can also wind itself through the motion of the wearer’s wrist.

I would rather build a small collection slowly and actually enjoy each watch. Keeping a few notes about what I like makes collecting feel more thoughtful, and it gives every piece a little story of its own.

Details I look for

  • A dial that is easy to read
  • A comfortable strap or bracelet
  • A case size that feels right on my wrist
  • A design I would actually wear often
