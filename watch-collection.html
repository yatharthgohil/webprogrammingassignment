03 / A Watch Collection

Different watches.
Different stories.

Collect slowly, choose thoughtfully, and let every piece earn a place through use and memory.

The journal

Built with meaning

A watch collection can start with just one piece that means something to you. A gift, a reminder of a special day, or a watch you saved up for can be more interesting than a drawer full of things you never wear.

Learning about movements is part of the fun. Quartz uses an electronic oscillator, while mechanical watches run on a wound spring. An automatic watch winds that spring through the motion of the wearer’s wrist.

I would rather build a small collection slowly and actually enjoy each watch. Keeping a few notes about what I like makes collecting feel more thoughtful, and it gives every piece a story of its own.

A closer look

Details I look for

  • A dial that is easy to read
  • A comfortable strap or bracelet
  • A case size that feels right
  • A design I would wear often
